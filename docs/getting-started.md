# Getting Started with Arch Tech

## What is Arch Tech?

Arch Tech is a project management platform for teams of 5–200 people. It helps you organize tasks, track deadlines, collaborate on documents, and monitor project progress — all in one place.

## Creating Your Account

1. Go to https://app.archtech.dev and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your work email and create a password (minimum 10 characters, must include a number)
3. Verify your email by clicking the link we send you
4. Choose your plan: Free, Pro, or Business (you can upgrade anytime)
5. Create your first workspace and invite your team

## Your First Project

### Step 1: Create a project
Click **+ New Project** in the sidebar. Give it a name, pick a color, and choose a template:
- **Blank** — start from scratch
- **Software Development** — columns: Backlog, In Progress, Review, Done
- **Marketing Campaign** — columns: Ideas, Planned, In Progress, Published
- **Client Onboarding** — columns: New Lead, Discovery, Proposal, Active

### Step 2: Add tasks
Click **+ Add Task** in any column. Each task can have:
- Title and description (Markdown supported)
- Assignee (one person or a team)
- Due date and time estimate
- Priority: Low, Medium, High, Urgent
- Labels (custom, color-coded)
- Subtasks (checklist)
- File attachments

### Step 3: Invite your team
Go to **Settings → Members → Invite**. Enter email addresses separated by commas. Each person gets a role:
- **Admin** — full access, can manage billing and members
- **Member** — can create/edit projects and tasks
- **Viewer** — read-only access, can leave comments

### Step 4: Set up notifications
Go to **Settings → Notifications**. You can receive alerts via:
- Email digest (daily or weekly summary)
- Push notifications (browser and mobile)
- Slack integration (posts to a channel when tasks change status)

## Mobile App

Arch Tech is available on iOS (14+) and Android (10+). Download from the App Store or Google Play. The mobile app supports:
- Viewing and editing tasks
- Taking photos and attaching them to tasks
- Push notifications
- Offline mode (syncs when back online)

The mobile app does not currently support: Gantt charts, custom automations, or admin settings. Use the web app for those features.

## Data & Security

- All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Servers are hosted on AWS in the EU (Frankfurt) and US (Virginia)
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- GDPR compliant — you can export or delete all your data at any time
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) available for all accounts
- SSO via SAML 2.0 (Business plan only)
- Daily backups with 30-day retention
- 99.9% uptime SLA (Business plan)
