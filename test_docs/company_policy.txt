Acme Corp — Customer Support Knowledge Base
============================================

1. RETURN POLICY
----------------
Customers may return any product within 30 calendar days of the delivery date.
The product must be in its original packaging, unused, and with all tags attached.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Shipping costs for returns are covered by Acme Corp for defective products only.
For non-defective returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.
A restocking fee of 15% applies to electronics returned after 14 days.

To initiate a return:
1. Log in to your Acme Corp account at acme-corp.com/returns
2. Select the order and item you wish to return
3. Print the prepaid shipping label (if eligible)
4. Ship the item within 7 days of initiating the return

2. SHIPPING INFORMATION
-----------------------
Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50.
Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $9.99 flat rate.
Overnight shipping: next business day, $19.99 flat rate.
International shipping: 10-15 business days, rates vary by destination.

All orders are shipped from our warehouse in Austin, Texas.
Tracking numbers are sent via email within 24 hours of shipment.
Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days ship the same day.

3. WARRANTY
-----------
All Acme Corp products come with a 2-year limited warranty.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures.
The warranty does NOT cover:
- Damage from drops, water, or misuse
- Normal wear and tear
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents)

To file a warranty claim:
1. Contact support@acme-corp.com with your order number
2. Describe the issue and attach photos
3. Our team will respond within 48 hours with next steps
