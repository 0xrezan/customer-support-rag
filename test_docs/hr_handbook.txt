Acme Corp — Employee HR Handbook (Abridged)
=============================================

1. WORKING HOURS
----------------
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
Core hours (mandatory presence) are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Flexible start: employees may begin between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
Lunch break: 1 hour, typically 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM, but flexible.
Total work week: 40 hours.
Overtime requires prior written approval from the direct manager.
Overtime is compensated at 1.5x the hourly rate for weekdays and 2x for weekends.

2. REMOTE WORK POLICY
----------------------
All employees are eligible for hybrid work after completing the 90-day probation period.
Remote work schedule: up to 3 days per week from home.
Fully remote positions are available for engineering and customer support roles.
Employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM).
Home office stipend: $500 one-time for new hires, $200/year for equipment refresh.
VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems remotely.
International remote work requires HR approval at least 30 days in advance.

3. PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
-----------------------
Annual PTO: 20 days for all full-time employees.
PTO accrues at 1.67 days per month.
Unused PTO carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for periods longer than 3 days.
Same-day PTO requests are allowed for emergencies with manager approval.
Public holidays: 11 company-observed holidays per year (see HR calendar).

4. SICK LEAVE
-------------
Sick leave: 10 days per year, separate from PTO.
Doctor's note required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Unused sick days do NOT carry over to the next year.
Mental health days count as sick leave — no doctor's note required for single days.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
-----------------
Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid at 100% salary.
Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid at 100% salary.
Adoption and surrogacy are covered under the same policy.
Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.
Gradual return: employees may work 50% schedule for 4 weeks after returning.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
----------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: January and July.
Self-assessment is due 2 weeks before the review meeting.
Manager assessment includes 360-degree feedback from at least 3 peers.
Rating scale: 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Exceptional).
Employees rated 4 or above are eligible for the annual bonus pool.
Performance Improvement Plans (PIP) are issued for ratings below 2 for two consecutive cycles.

7. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
-----------------------------
Salary reviews: annually in March, effective April 1st.
Annual bonus: up to 15% of base salary, based on individual and company performance.
Stock options: available for employees at Senior level and above, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
Health insurance: 100% premium covered for employees, 75% for dependents.
Dental and vision: included in the health plan at no additional cost.
401(k): company matches 100% up to 4% of salary.
Education budget: $2,000/year for courses, conferences, and certifications.
Gym membership: $50/month reimbursement.

8. CODE OF CONDUCT
------------------
All employees must complete annual compliance training by December 31st.
Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, and retaliation.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR within 30 days.
Gifts from vendors or clients exceeding $100 must be reported to the Ethics Committee.
Social media: do not share confidential company information publicly.
Violation of the code of conduct may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

9. RESIGNATION AND OFFBOARDING
-------------------------------
Notice period: 2 weeks for individual contributors, 4 weeks for managers.
Exit interview is conducted by HR on the last working day.
Company equipment (laptop, monitors, badges) must be returned within 5 business days.
Final paycheck includes unused PTO balance, paid out at the current daily rate.
Non-compete clause: 6 months post-employment for senior and executive roles.
Access to company systems is revoked at 5:00 PM on the last working day.
